# Third-party 3D models in Fuzzies Land

All third-party models are **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** — free for commercial use, no attribution required. We credit the
creators anyway. Each pack's own licence file was checked at download (`tools/assets/fetch_sources.sh` lists them).

| Pack | Creator | Licence | Used in | Where it came from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stylized Nature MegaKit (free standard version) | Quaternius — https://quaternius.com | CC0 1.0 (`License_Standard.txt` in the pack) | `models/kits/orchard*.glb`: trees, pines, bushes, ferns, flowers, grass, clover, mushrooms, rocks, pebbles | the creator's free zip, via a GitHub release mirror (github.com/Noisemaker111/jgengine, release `packs`) |
| Medieval Village MegaKit (free standard version) | Quaternius — https://quaternius.com | CC0 1.0 (`License_Standard.txt` in the pack) | `models/kits/orchard*.glb`: the farmhouse (assembled from wall, window, door, roof and chimney modules), fence, wagon, crates | as above |
| KayKit Restaurant Bits 1.0 | Kay Lousberg — https://www.kaylousberg.com | CC0 1.0 (`LICENSE.txt` in the repository) | fetched for the kitchen / farm kits (not shipped yet) | github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Restaurant-Bits-1.0 |

Our own models (no licence needed): the fruit (apple, pear, strawberry, golden apple), the pinecone and the woven basket in
`orchard*.glb` (`tools/assets/props.py`). The hero Fuzzy is drawn in code (`src/game/fuzzyHero.ts`) after the owner's own picture.
